## Customer registration policy

The current messaging business slice remains intentionally limited to `RegisterNewCustomer` with V2 minimum:

```text
customer.name
customer.contact.phone
customer.contact.email
```

On verified WhatsApp consent, provider sender identity may seed the phone candidate. Customer/Temporal still owns validation and completeness; WhatsApp must not ask for the phone again if Temporal already knows it.
