# MK1 — Current Architecture Snapshot

Date: 2026-09-04
Branch: `design/mk1-telegram-whatsapp-official-channels`

## Why this snapshot exists

We intentionally moved one step back from Services/Scheduler construction to finish the messaging-channel architecture for two transports:

- Telegram;
- WhatsApp.

This is a design and documentation checkpoint. It does **not** revoke or rewrite previously certified work.

## Core architecture rule

**Engines comes first. CTA is independent and replaceable.**

CLI, HTTP/Postman, WebChat, Telegram and WhatsApp are peer ingress surfaces. They are only different ways for a client to connect to the same Engine.

```text
CLI ───────────┐
HTTP/API ──────┤
WebChat ───────┤
Telegram ──────┤── CTA boundary ── ENGINES
WhatsApp ──────┘
```

Telegram and WhatsApp therefore do not add business engines. They add transport and connection adapters only.

Normative design: [`Design/11-engine-first-cta-independence.md`](Design/11-engine-first-cta-independence.md).

## Channel invariant already proven by C1B

```text
Client transport
      ↓
CTA / ChannelAdapter
      ↓
CanonicalChannelEnvelope
      ↓
ChannelExecutionCore
      ↓
PostgreSQL durable channel ledger
      ↓
Temporal
      ↓
existing Engine Workflow Library
```

The new Telegram and WhatsApp work must reuse this path. Neither channel may introduce direct business writes, provider-specific Customer/Services/Scheduler policy, or a second source of conversation truth.

## Current certified durable semantics

For a canonical inbound event:

```text
same inbound identity + same normalized material
→ durable replay
→ no second business effect

same inbound identity + different normalized material
→ CHANNEL_EVENT_IDENTITY_CONFLICT
→ no Workflow mutation

adapter process restart
→ recover conversation binding from PostgreSQL
→ continue the same Temporal Workflow
```

Those semantics are transport-independent and are the base contract for Telegram and WhatsApp.

## Current messaging slice

The first Telegram/WhatsApp business Workflow is intentionally limited to:

```text
RegisterNewCustomer
```

Target interaction:

```text
first contact / interactive campaign reply
→ explicit permission to register
→ start RegisterNewCustomer in Temporal
→ query missingFields / nextAction
→ collect only missing Customer data through the channel
→ explicit finalize
→ Customer completion
```

Appointment, advisor handoff, promotional campaign scheduling and other Workflows are deferred from this first slice.

## Important Customer policy boundary

The currently certified golden registration policy requires:

```text
customer.name
customer.contact.phoneOrEmail
```

The desired future messaging experience discussed on 2026-09-04 expects:

```text
customer.name
customer.contact.phone
customer.contact.email
```

That stricter minimum is **not currently an Engine invariant**.

It must not be hardcoded in WhatsApp or Telegram. If adopted, `RegistrationPolicy` must be evolved and certified in a separate Customer Engine gate so every CTA surface observes the same `missingFields` truth.

## Transport-specific prefill

### WhatsApp

The official provider sender identity may seed a phone candidate after explicit registration acceptance when the selected official provider contract marks the sender value as suitable and it passes canonical Customer validation.

If the phone is already accepted into Workflow state, the channel must not ask the customer to type it again.

### Telegram

Telegram chat/user IDs are transport identities, not phone numbers.

Phone must come from explicit contact sharing or typed input. Telegram profile names may be treated as hints rather than silently persisted canonical Customer names.

## Current Step-3 truth

Services S7 is certified on `build/mk1-s7-appointment-services-integration`.

Appointment consumes canonical Services reads and freezes revision-aware Service/Offering selection state while preserving the inherited Appointment completion path.

Scheduler authority remains separate. S7 did not move concrete availability, resources, holds, reservations or conflicts into Services.

## Current focus

The active design focus is now:

```text
C2 Telegram — RegisterNewCustomer CTA transport
C4 WhatsApp — RegisterNewCustomer CTA transport
```

Physical provider testing is intentionally deferred until the architecture and adapters are ready.

## Non-negotiable WhatsApp requirement

We will not use browser automation, WhatsApp Web session emulation, QR-session scraping, reverse-engineered clients, or any other unofficial transport that risks account/number enforcement.

The WhatsApp path must remain on an official WhatsApp Business Platform/API route.

Meta direct vs Kapso remains an implementation decision behind `WhatsAppTransportPort`; it does not alter Engine ownership.
